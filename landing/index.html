Three steps

From a URL to a published article.

1

Read the site

Crawls your public URL and summarises what the business is and which topics it covers. That summary grounds everything that follows.

2

Research

Searches and collects current information in that field and compiles a sourced report. You can also steer it with a keyword.

3

Publish

Turns the findings into a readable article and publishes it on your site. Manual posting is available too.

Why it stays close to your site

Auto-posting unrelated articles only makes a site noisy. Research is always grounded in your site's own summary, so what gets published sits on the same line as your business. Numbers and names that are not in the research are never invented.

One file to install

  • You place a single display-only PHP file. No database, no backend on your server.
  • Articles live on our side; a issued key lets your page read only your own posts.
  • A latest-posts block for existing pages is included, and RSS is served.
  • Posting and editing happen only in our admin screen, so no write access sits on your site.
